Episode Studio

Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

About Terms of Service Privacy Policy

Episode Studio is a desktop app with no server of its own. It does not send your information to the developer.

What the app uses

  • From TikTok, with your permission: your basic profile (open ID, display name, username and avatar) and your posting options (privacy choices, interaction settings and maximum video length). The app uses these to show which account it will post to and which options you can choose.
  • Your videos and captions: the files you select, and the captions and hashtags you write, are sent directly from your computer to TikTok when you choose to upload.
  • Sign-in details: the TikTok access token and your developer app keys are saved on your computer, encrypted for your Windows account.
  • Upload records: lists of uploaded episodes, caption settings and a scheduling log are saved as files in your own video folders.

What the app does not do

  • It has no analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • It does not sell, rent or share your information with anyone other than TikTok.
  • It does not read your TikTok messages, followers or other videos.

Where your data goes

Information is exchanged only between your computer and TikTok's servers. TikTok's own privacy policy covers what TikTok does with your videos and account data.

Keeping and deleting data

Everything the app saves stays on your computer until you delete it. To disconnect, click Disconnect in the app, or remove Episode Studio in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, then Security and permissions, then Apps and services permissions. Deleting the app's .auth folder removes the saved sign-in.

Children

Episode Studio is not meant for anyone who is not old enough to use TikTok where they live.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will show the new effective date.

Episode Studio is an independent app. It is not made, sponsored or endorsed by TikTok.
