Episode Studio

Episode Studio helps a creator post the microseries episodes they own or are licensed to post to their own TikTok account, in episode order, through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

How it works

Episode Studio runs only on the creator's Windows PC. There is no Episode Studio server or online account. The creator connects their TikTok account with TikTok's Login Kit, chooses a folder of episodes, and posts them in order with captions such as "Episode 12/60". They can also send episodes to their TikTok drafts to finish in the TikTok app, or schedule a few new episodes each day.

  • Before each upload the app shows which TikTok account will receive the post.
  • The creator chooses who can view each post from the options TikTok allows, with no default.
  • Comments, duets and stitches stay off unless the creator turns them on.
  • The creator confirms they own the videos or have the rights holder's written permission before anything is sent.
